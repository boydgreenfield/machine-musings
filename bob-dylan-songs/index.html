Bob Dylan

    How to read this

    Every card is one song, hand-lettered: 450 studio album tracks plus 84 originals that never made an album. The card's colour is the era. Its size is the song's length. Titles in black marker are Dylan originals; titles in pencil grey are covers and traditionals.

    Highway
    Highway 61, album by album. Each song is a sign on a stake by the road; the taller the stake, the longer the song. Singles and outtakes line the far shoulder at the year they were recorded.
    Years
    A column of cards for every year from 1961 to 2020, stacked in release order.
    Records
    A wall of vinyl. Each album is a record with its songs fanned around the rim; a song's place on the rim is its share of the running time.
    Lexicon
    The sixteen words Dylan put in titles most often, with the songs that carry them.
