Bob Dylan

    How to read this

    Every mark is one song: 450 studio album tracks plus 85 originals that never made an album. Colour is the era. Size is the song's length. Dimmer marks are covers and traditionals; the brightest marks are Dylan originals.

    Galaxy
    A spiral of time. Each album is a cluster of stars on the arm; non-album songs drift in the halo.
    Highway
    Highway 61, album by album. Each song stands on the roadside; height is its length. Singles and outtakes line the far shoulder at the year they were recorded.
    Records
    A wall of vinyl. Each album is a record; each song's arc of the groove is proportional to its share of the running time.
    Lexicon
    The sixteen words Dylan put in titles most often, with the songs that carry them.
