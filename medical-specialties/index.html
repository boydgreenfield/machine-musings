      How to read the atlas

      Medicine sits at the centre. Around it are nine broad domains, then the specialties that have their own residency and board, then the subspecialties reached by fellowship. Colour is the domain; size is the level. Dashed arcs are cross-board pathways: fellowships you can enter from more than one specialty.
