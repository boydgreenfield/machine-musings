    The Atlas of Medicine

    Medicine is not one profession but a constellation of them. This map shows every primary specialty recognised by the American Board of Medical Specialties, the subspecialties that grow from each, and the fields that bridge several boards at once.

    • Switch layouts to see the same fields as orbits, a physics constellation, a tower of training years, or body systems.

    About the data

    The taxonomy follows the 24 member boards of the American Board of Medical Specialties (ABMS) and their certificates, with ACGME-accredited or widely recognised society fellowships added where they shape practice. Training lengths are typical minimums after medical school. Workforce figures are approximate counts of active US physicians, rounded from public AAMC and ABMS reports, and used only for relative sizing. Osteopathic (AOA) boards, non-US systems, and combined residencies other than Med-Peds are not shown.
